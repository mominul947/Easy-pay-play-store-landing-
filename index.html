About this app arrow_forward

Easy Pay is the Digital Financial Service of Bangladesh.

The Easy Pay app is a fast, secure, and reliable digital financial service app that makes your everyday transactions simple such as Cash In, Cash Out, Send Money (P2P), Mobile Recharge, and more. You ll receive notifications of your latest activities, and you can easily view your transaction history and summaries.

Easy Pay will soon bring exciting and innovative services to meet customer needs and expectations.

Key Features:

Secure Transactions: The Easy Pay app is highly secure and requires your PIN at two different stages for extra safety.

Personalization: Add your display name and change your profile picture anytime.

Error-Free Money Transfer: Select recipients directly from your contact list to avoid sending money to the wrong number.

  • Cash In and Cash Out
  • Send Money (P2P)
  • Mobile Recharge
  • Bill Payments
  • Transaction History
